# Aqua Patrol

What Is AquaPatrol?
AquaPatrol is a small autonomous boat that patrols a fish pond on its own, continuously measuring pH and
temperature across the entire water surface — not just one spot — and reporting everything back to a live
web dashboard.
It replaces manual water testing with a system that covers the whole pond, runs on a schedule you set, and
gives farmers a real-time picture of water conditions from their phone.

The Problem:
Water quality is the single biggest risk factor in fish farming. A silent pH crash or temperature swing can
wipe out an entire pond's stock before anyone notices.
How ponds are monitored today
• One sample point: a worker dips a handheld meter at one or two spots — a pond is not uniform, so this
misses most of the water.
• Infrequent checks: typically once a day at best, leaving long windows where a dangerous shift can go
undetected.
• Manual labor dependency: the entire system relies on a person remembering to check, every day, in
every weather condition.
• No historical record: readings often live in a notebook, if they're recorded at all — no trends, no early
warning.

How It Works:
• Plan a route: draw a patrol boundary and waypoints on the web dashboard, from a phone or computer.
• Autonomous navigation: the boat uses GPS and a compass to follow the route on its own — no remote
control needed.
• Continuous sensing: pH and temperature sensors take readings throughout the patrol, tagged with exact
GPS position.
• Loop mode: missions can run once, or loop back and forth continuously for ongoing monitoring.
• Live data: every reading streams back to the dashboard in real time over WiFi.
• Full-pond mapping: the dashboard interpolates all the readings into a complete color-coded map of the
pond, plus trend graphs over time.

Key Features:
• Fully autonomous GPS waypoint navigation
• Live pH and temperature heatmap of the entire pond
• Historical trend graphs (24h / 7 days / all time)
• Mission modes: run once, or loop continuously
• Draw a no-go boundary so the boat stays inside the pond
• Remote abort — stop the boat instantly from the dashboard
• Mobile-friendly web dashboard — works from any phone on the network
• Multi-device support — monitor from more than one phone or computer at once
• Runs on a local network — no cloud subscription required to operate

Business Opportunities:
• Hardware sales: sell ready-to-deploy patrol boats directly to individual farms.
• Monitoring-as-a-service: a subscription covering the dashboard, data storage, and support — recurring
revenue.
• Fleet management: larger operations running many ponds can manage an entire fleet of boats from one
dashboard.
• Data & consulting: the water quality history this system collects supports feeding, stocking, and yield
decisions — valuable on its own.
The cost to build and deploy one unit is a small fraction of what a single water-quality crisis can cost a farm
in lost stock — making the return on investment straightforward to demonstrate.
